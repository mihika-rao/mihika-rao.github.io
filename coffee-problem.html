SEGMENTATION

The same customer can look different by occasion.

THE MINIMALIST$4–7

Drip coffee, no add-ons, walk there. The control group of coffee behavior.

THE RITUAL$10–16

Specialty drink + alt milk + tip. Predictable, repeatable, and therefore analytically important.

THE WHOLE OCCASION$18–30

Drink + food + tip. Basket-size growth is mostly outside the beverage itself.

THE I’M LATE TAX$30+

Transportation dominates the order. A “coffee” purchase becomes a convenience-spend event.

This is the analytical habit I wanted the project to show: define the unit correctly before you optimize it. If the business only studies beverage price, it misses the behavior around the beverage.
