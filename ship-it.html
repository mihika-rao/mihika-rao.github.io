THE DECISION FRAME

A good experiment has more than one success condition.

01Primary metric

What behavior are we explicitly trying to move?

02Guardrails

What cannot get materially worse while we optimize?

03Practical effect

Is the uplift large enough to matter operationally or economically?

04Uncertainty

How much evidence do we have, and where is the interval?

05Mechanism

Do we understand why the treatment worked — and why anything else moved?
