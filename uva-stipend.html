02 / BENCHMARKING + SURVEY ANALYTICS + STORYTELLING

Evidence that moved

A compensation question was being argued with anecdotes. I helped turn it into a measurable case with peer benchmarks, student survey evidence, and a story leadership could act on.

THE QUESTION

How do you make an institutional problem legible?

Not “are stipends low?” but: low relative to what, for whom, and with what consequences?

I combined external benchmarking with internal survey evidence so the argument wasn’t dependent on a single metric. One layer established market context; another showed lived outcomes; cost-of-living analysis connected the two.

THE STORY

From analysis to a room full of decision-makers.

The analytical work was only half the job. I built visualizations linking compensation to cost of living, reported stress, and self-reported productivity, then translated the evidence into a decision-ready narrative for university leadership.

The goal was not to overwhelm with every cut of the data. It was to make the central tradeoff impossible to miss.
