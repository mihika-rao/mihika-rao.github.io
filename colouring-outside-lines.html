THE IDEA

A colouring book for people whose brains have seventeen tabs open.

The pages are built around ordinary scenes that become weirdly personal: the desk you keep promising to clean, the market tote that is somehow always too small, the apartment plant that has become a dependent, the window seat you booked six months early.

No productivity system. No lesson. Just a reason to make a mess with colour.

THE BOOK

Black lines first.
Everything else is negotiable.

The book moves through three chapters: staying in, going out, and escaping somewhere else. Each spread pairs a short observation with a dense line illustration designed to be coloured slowly—or aggressively.
